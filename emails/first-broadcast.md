# First broadcast

Send this a few days after launch, once there are people on the list. It is the proof email:
it shows what a real alert looks like, so subscribers learn the format and learn that a quiet
inbox is deliberate.

Do not send it the same day people subscribe. They already got the welcome email with the same
three items in it. Let a gap open first, or it reads as spam.

---

**Subject:** Windsurf swapped your monthly budget for a daily speed limit

---

One change this week that is worth five minutes of your attention, and it is not the one that
got the headlines.

**Windsurf replaced monthly credits with daily and weekly quotas, and moved Pro from $15 to $20.**

The price rise is the small part. The real change is structural: a quota is a rate limit, not a
budget. Under the old system, Pro gave you 500 credits a month and you could spend all of them
in one weekend on one hard project. Under the new system the allowance refreshes daily and
weekly, so there is no pool left to drain.

If your work is steady, you will barely notice. If your work is bursty, which describes most
people shipping a product, this constrains you in a way the old plan did not, and the $5 does
not tell you that.

Existing paid subscribers keep their old pricing. Worth checking which side of that line you
are on before you let a subscription lapse and re-subscribe, because re-subscribing puts you
on the new terms.

Source, with the date: https://devin.ai/blog/windsurf-pricing-plans

**Also logged since the last time you heard from me**

Anthropic cancelled a scheduled Sonnet 5 price increase. It was due to go from $2/$10 to
$3/$15 per million tokens on 1 September, and was withdrawn on 11 August. Logged because a
price rise that gets announced and then pulled changes your decisions the same way a real one
does, and nobody sends an email when a rise is cancelled.

Lovable merged its two billing balances into one credit pool. The headline allocation went up,
but multi-file generations that used to cost 1 credit now cost 2 to 3. Count credits per
finished feature, not per message, when you compare against your old bill.
