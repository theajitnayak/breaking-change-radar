# Welcome email

Paste into Buttondown under **Settings → Emails → Welcome email**, sent automatically once
someone confirms. Keep it short. The job is to set expectations so nobody is surprised later,
and to deliver value in the first thirty seconds so the subscribe feels worth it.

---

**Subject:** You will hear from me when your bill is about to change

---

Thanks for subscribing.

Here is exactly what you signed up for, so there are no surprises:

**You get an email when a tool you pay for changes what it charges, what it allows, or what it
removes.** Price changes, credit allowances, rate limits, quotas, deprecations with a date on them.

**You do not get a newsletter.** No weekly digest, no roundup, no "10 AI tools you should try."
If nothing changes for two weeks, you hear nothing for two weeks. That is the product working,
not the product failing.

Three things already in the tracker that are worth knowing today:

**Replit Agent no longer has a price you can see before you commit.** It moved off flat $0.25
checkpoints to effort-based pricing in July. The agent decides what the task cost, and you find
out after the run. People report being billed on runs that errored.

**Claude Haiku 3 retired, and the default replacement is four times the input price.** $0.25 to
$1.00 per million tokens. If you have a high-volume classification or extraction job that was
sized on Haiku 3 economics, check Haiku 3.5 at $0.80 before you accept the default migration
to 4.5.

**Gemini Pro models left the free API tier on 1 April.** Anything running on free Pro access
stopped that day unless billing was attached.

The full tracker is here, and every entry cites the vendor page it came from with its date:
https://theajitnayak.github.io/breaking-change-radar/

One more thing worth knowing about how this works. When I cannot confirm something against a
vendor's own page, I still publish it, marked UNVERIFIED, with whatever source I do have. A
low-confidence item you can see and check yourself beats a confident one you cannot. About
seven of the sixteen entries carry that mark right now.

If you spot something wrong, the data is a public repo and corrections are pull requests:
https://github.com/theajitnayak/breaking-change-radar

And if you got burned by a pricing change I have not logged, just reply to this email. That is
genuinely the most useful thing you can send me.

Ajit
